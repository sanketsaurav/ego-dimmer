## [1.0.1] - 2026-08-24

The first public release of Ego Dimmer brings bright HDR media back to a comfortable range without blocking or replacing it.

### Added

- Per-site HDR dimming for images, videos, and canvases, with LinkedIn, X, and Twitter enabled by default.
- A simple current-site switch for adding or removing dimming on other websites.
- Two intensity options: Full SDR removes extra HDR brightness, while Gentle keeps a little headroom.
- Chrome Sync support for enabled websites and intensity preferences.

### Privacy

- No analytics, tracking, advertising, accounts, remote code, or developer-operated servers.
- Optional website access is requested only when dimming is enabled for that site and revoked when it is disabled.
- The complete source is available under the MIT License.
